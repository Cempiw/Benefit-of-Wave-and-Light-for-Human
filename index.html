🌊 What is a Wave?

A wave is a vibration that transfers energy from one place to another. Waves can move through water, air, and even space.

  • Mechanical waves need medium
  • Electromagnetic waves do not need medium
  • Examples: ocean waves, sound, light, radio

📡 Wave Characteristics

Amplitude

Height of wave. Bigger amplitude = bigger energy.

Frequency

Number of waves every second.

Wavelength

Distance between two crests.

Wave Speed

How fast wave travels.

v = fλ

🏥 Waves in Medical Technology

  • Ultrasound for pregnancy check
  • X-Ray for bones
  • Laser surgery for eyes
  • MRI body scanning

Frequency and wavelength affect how medical tools work.

📱 Waves in Communication

  • Wi-Fi uses radio waves
  • Mobile phones use electromagnetic waves
  • Satellite sends information worldwide
  • Bluetooth transfers data wirelessly
