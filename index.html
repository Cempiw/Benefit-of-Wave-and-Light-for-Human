🌊 What is a Wave?

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another. Waves can travel through water, air, solids, and even space!

  • Mechanical Waves → need medium
  • Electromagnetic Waves → no medium needed
  • Examples: sound, ocean waves, radio waves, light

📡 Wave Characteristics

Waves have four important characteristics:

Amplitude
Frequency
Wavelength
Wave Speed

v = fλ

Wave speed depends on frequency and wavelength.

🏥 Waves in Medical Technology

Waves help doctors save lives!

  • Ultrasound → checking baby development
  • X-Ray → seeing bones
  • Laser surgery → eye treatment
  • MRI → body scanning

Different frequencies create different uses and safety levels.

📱 Waves in Communication

Communication technology works because of electromagnetic waves.

  • Wi-Fi
  • Mobile phones
  • Radio
  • Satellite
  • Bluetooth

These technologies help people connect around the world.

⚠️ Technology & Safety

Technology is useful, but it must be used responsibly.

  • Too much radiation can be dangerous
  • People debate about 5G tower placement
  • Scientists study safety carefully
  • Communities and scientists must work together

🧠 Mini Experiment

  1. Take a rope or slinky.
  2. Move it slowly and observe the wave.
  3. Move it faster and compare the wavelength.
  4. Discuss: What happens when frequency increases?

🌍 Waves & Sustainable Development Goals

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-Being

Waves are used in medical technologies such as ultrasound, laser surgery, MRI, and X-ray. These technologies help doctors diagnose diseases and save lives.

SDG 9

Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Communication systems such as radio, internet, satellite, and mobile networks depend on wave technology. Waves support global innovation and modern infrastructure.

SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals

Scientists, governments, engineers, and communities must work together to ensure technology is safe and beneficial for society.

🌿 Blue & Green Curriculum

🌊 Blue Curriculum

Blue Curriculum focuses on ocean literacy and sustainability. Students learn how waves, oceans, and marine ecosystems affect human life.

  • Ocean awareness
  • Marine conservation
  • Climate understanding
  • Environmental responsibility

🌱 Green Curriculum

Green Curriculum integrates sustainability and climate education into learning. It encourages students to become environmentally responsible citizens.

  • Climate education
  • Sustainable lifestyle
  • Problem solving
  • Eco-friendly innovation

💡 Why Is This Important?

By learning about waves and light through sustainability education, students not only understand science concepts but also understand how science can help protect people, technology, and the environment.

Students become future problem solvers who can use science responsibly.
