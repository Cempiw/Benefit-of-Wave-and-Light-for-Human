Driving Question:

🧠 Investigation

Definition: Wave is energy transfer without matter movement.

Wave Equation

v = f × λ

Where:
v = wave speed
f = frequency
λ = wavelength

🧪 Experiment

Use rope/slinky to observe wave motion.

Analysis Questions (HOTS)
